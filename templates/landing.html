How It Works

From problem to resolution in minutes

1

Describe your software issue

Tell the AI chatbot what's going wrong in plain language — no technical jargon required.

2

AI diagnoses and recommends solutions

ResolveAI classifies your issue, searches the knowledge base, and provides tailored troubleshooting steps.

3

Try the solution

Follow the recommended steps. The AI walks you through each one and answers follow-up questions.

4

Confirm resolution or escalate

Tell us whether it worked. If it did, the ticket is resolved. If not, a support engineer takes over immediately.

5

Engineer follow-up (if needed)

For escalated issues, assigned engineers get full context and history, resolving issues faster with no repeated explanations.
